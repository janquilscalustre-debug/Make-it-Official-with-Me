Dear Langga, ♡

Hello, Langga. ❤️

It's been 2 going 3 months since we started talking again, right? And honestly, I don't think I can hold it any longer. I want to make us official. It's a risk I'm willing to take because I know what I want, and it's you. I want to scream na you are mine, I want to have this feeling na ma-assure ako na sa akin ka at the end of the day. I want to be called "yours" without constantly asking myself, "Ano ba talaga ako sa kanya?" I want us to have a label, not as "besties" or "more than friends, less than lovers," but as lovers. I want to be your girlfriend, and I want you to be my boyfriend, without having to question where we stand in each other's lives.

And I know that this is a huge risk for us, especially because of what happened months ago. I know na hindi basta-basta mawawala yung mga nangyari before, and I understand if there are still doubts or fears. But I want to change that. I want you to know na seryoso ako sa'yo, na ikaw lang, and that I won't let anyone interfere with us this time. I want to prove to you na things can be different now, not just through my words but also through my actions. I don't want us to go through the same things again, and if ever we encounter problems, I want us to handle them better this time.

I also want you to feel na you can express your feelings to me freely. If there's something that bothers you, if may unfair sa situation natin, or if there's something na hindi mo nagugustuhan, I want you to tell me. I don't want you to keep everything to yourself just because you're scared of how I'll react. Especially with our situation na isang beses lang tayo nagkikita sa isang week, I know na it's unfair sometimes. I know na there are moments when we want to spend more time together but can't, and I don't want you to feel like you have to settle for that without even telling me how you feel. I want us to communicate and understand each other, even when things get difficult.

I know that making us official won't magically make everything easier. We'll still have our own problems, misunderstandings, and moments when things won't go as planned. But I want us to try, Langga. I want us to give each other the assurance and commitment that we've been missing. I want us to be honest with each other, to communicate instead of assuming things, and to fix whatever problems we may have instead of letting them ruin what we're trying to build. I don't expect us to be perfect, but I want us to be willing to learn and grow together.

And honestly, after all these months of talking again, I don't want to keep wondering what we could be if we just took the risk. I don't want to keep these feelings to myself when I know that what I want is to be with you. I want to experience things with you as your girlfriend, to make memories with you, and to know that we're both choosing each other despite everything that happened before. I'm willing to take that risk because I believe that we deserve a chance to make things right this time.

So, Langga, I want to ask you if you're willing to take this step with me. I want us to finally make things official, not because I'm forcing you or because I want an answer just for the sake of having a label, but because I genuinely want to be with you. I want us to be sure of each other, to choose each other, and to make this work together. I want to love you without having to question what I am to you, and I want you to feel the same assurance from me.

I love you, Langga, and I don't want to keep pretending na okay lang ako sa ganitong setup when, deep inside, I know that I want more with you. I want you to be mine, and I want to be yours, not just through words but through the commitment we're both willing to give.

I want us to be lovers, Langga. And if you're willing to take that risk with me, please tell me.

Always with love, ♡
